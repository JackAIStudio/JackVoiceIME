<p>JackVoice is a voice-first keyboard: tap any text field and you are greeted with "Tap to speak". What you say lands directly in the field — no copy and paste. Keyboard and voice panel are one app, with nothing else to install.</p>
<p>It offers both cloud and on-device recognition. On-device recognition runs on sherpa-onnx and works fully offline. Every recording is written to disk as it happens, so a failed transcription never loses your audio and you can retry at any time.</p>
<p>The keyboard is built on the open-source Trime (RIME engine), keeping Pinyin, Chinese/English switching, punctuation levels and emoji suggestions, with the layout redesigned for voice input.</p>
